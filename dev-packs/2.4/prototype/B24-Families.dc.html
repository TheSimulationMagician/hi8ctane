SPORT FAMILIES
Five families, each with its palette colour (wind has two shades: kite steel, wing navy). A new sport joins a family: it gets that colour, its own Solid Glyph icon, and the family's condition check. The finder, cards and map never change. Solid chips are live now, dashed ones come later.

FOR THE DEVELOPER
Sports live in a sports table (slug, family, name, icon, min level), not in code. Each family has one condition function: wind, waves, flat water, wheels, rock + trail. Indoor sports skip the forecast and use opening hours instead. Adding hiking = one row + one icon.
