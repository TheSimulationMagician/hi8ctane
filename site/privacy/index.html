PRIVACY

Draft. Francisco checks it, and a lawyer reviews it before the app launches. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who we are

Hi8ctane is run by Francisco Arredondo, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Questions and requests: hello@hi8ctane.com.

What we keep and why

  • Getting in: your email, your city and your founding plate number. To keep your place and your plate, and to tell you when crews start near you.
  • Your rider profile: your rider name, sports and the level you picked in each, your start point, how far you'll go and how you get there. To match you with crews at your level that you can reach.
  • New cities: the cities you said you're in, and if you'll captain, your sport and days. To count riders and captains per city and open it when there are enough.
  • Founding Night: your session, pickup hub, when you ticked the waiver, and whether photos of you are fine. To run the day and the night.
  • Schools and rental shops: the name, spot, what you offer and how to reach you. To get back to you.
  • Where you came from: if you arrived through an ad or someone's link (for example ?ref=), we note that with your sign-up, to know which links work.
  • Crews: the crews you start or join, your level and how you get there (driving, a seat, your own way), and the crew chat. Only people in that crew see the names, the cars, the meeting note and the chat.
  • At the spot: when you check in and the reports you post (on, meh or off, your kite size or the ground). Your crew sees your name and note; everyone else sees the report without your name.
  • Settling up: what your car cost and, if you add them, a payment link or your Bizum number. Only the riders in your car see them; your Bizum number is never sent by email.
  • Alerts: the sports and cities you want an email about when a crew is posted. Every email has a stop link.

Your email is never shown to other riders. Later, other riders will see your rider name, level and plate.

In your browser

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed up from page to page, the site keeps a random key and your choices in your browser's local storage, and the weight you give for the kite size stays there too: it never leaves your phone. Clearing this site's data removes them; it doesn't delete your sign-up.

Stopping spam

To stop bots filling the lists, we count sign-ups per visitor using a scrambled (salted hash) version of your IP address, never the address itself, and delete it after a day.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase stores the data, in the EU.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website; like any web host, it sees your IP address.
  • Open-Meteo provides the forecast: your browser asks it directly, so it sees your IP address, not who you are.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on a few older pages (Founding Night, cities, schools), so Google sees your IP address there. The app itself uses fonts we host.
  • Mixcloud plays Hi8 Radio, only when you press play.
  • Email: when we send you a sign-in link or news about your city, it goes out through our email provider.

We never sell your data.

Legal basis

Doing what you asked (keeping your place, your plate, your RSVP), your consent (photos), and our legitimate interest in keeping the lists free of spam.

How long

Until you ask us to delete it, or 24 months after we last heard from you. The spam counters after a day.

Your rights

Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time: email hello@hi8ctane.com from the address you signed up with, and we do it within 30 days. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Age

Hi8ctane is for people aged 18 and over.
