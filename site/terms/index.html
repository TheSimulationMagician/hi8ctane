TERMS

Draft for the founding list, city lists and the Founding Night RSVP. The app's full terms come before it launches. Last updated 2 November 2026.

A preview

Hi8ctane isn't running crews yet. The crews, spots and riders you see in the app screens are examples of how it will work. Signing up saves your place, your plate and your choices for when crews start in your city.

Free

Joining the list, getting a plate and RSVPing are free. Rides on Hi8ctane will be cost-sharing only: fuel and parking split evenly, settled with the driver, with no cut for us.

Founding plates

The first 100 people on each city's list get a numbered plate. Plates are personal and can't be sold or transferred. We may withdraw a plate that's used to spam or abuse others.

Your own call

Kite, wing, surf, SUP, MTB and MX carry real risk. The forecast is a model, not a beach sensor. Every session, every crew and every ride is your own call, and you must be 18 or over. Captains are riders who host and welcome people; they are not instructors and don't teach. Kite only after a course.

Founding Night

The date, venue and sessions can change with the weather or the venue. Plan B goes out 3 hours before. Without the waiver tick you can come to the night but not the sessions.
